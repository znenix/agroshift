# Uzbek and Russian proofreading list

Generated by `node scripts/i18n-proofread.mjs` from `locales/*.json`. The Uzbek and Russian texts were drafted with AI and **must be proofread by native speakers on the team** (SPEC §7) before the demo.

**How to fix a text:**
- Edit `locales/uz.json` or `locales/ru.json`, not this file.
- Keep every `{placeholder}` exactly as it is; `npm test` checks this.
- Uzbek must stay in Latin script, with `ʻ` (U+02BB) in oʻ/gʻ and `ʼ` (U+02BC) for the tutuq belgisi.
- Then run this script again.

**Proofread first** (on screen during the 30-second demo): `field`, `map`, `conditions`, `farm`, `compare`, `satellite`, `stress`, `indicator`, `status`, `confidence`.

## Known issues to check

- **Soil texture classes** are USDA classes (`texture.*`). Local soil science uses the Kachinsky classification, so the names are approximate. Check that they read sensibly, or switch to a short description.
- **Russian plurals:** `value.days_year`, `unit.*` and `trend.detail` use one form ("дней") for every number; `value.seasons` avoids the problem as "сезонов: {value}". Rephrase if needed.
- **Russian city phrases:** `field.choose.near` and `conditions.location` say "рядом с городом {city}" and "… км от города {city}" so that the city name can stay in the nominative case.

| Key | English | Oʻzbekcha | Русский |
|---|---|---|---|
| `glossary.ndvi` | NDVI (Normalised Difference Vegetation Index) measures how green the land is from space: about 0.1 for bare soil, 0.3–0.5 for a young crop and 0.6–0.9 for a dense green crop. | NDVI (normallashtirilgan vegetatsiya indeksi) yerning koinotdan qanchalik yashil koʻrinishini oʻlchaydi: ochiq tuproq uchun taxminan 0,1, yosh ekin uchun 0,3–0,5, zich yashil ekin uchun 0,6–0,9. | NDVI (нормализованный разностный вегетационный индекс) показывает, насколько зелёной выглядит земля из космоса: около 0,1 для голой почвы, 0,3–0,5 для молодых посевов и 0,6–0,9 для густых зелёных посевов. |
| `glossary.salinity` | Salinity is measured as the electrical conductivity of a saturated soil paste (ECe, dS/m). Above about 4 dS/m many crops lose yield; cotton and barley tolerate more than beans or maize. | Shoʻrlanish toʻyingan tuproq pastasining elektr oʻtkazuvchanligi (ECe, dS/m) bilan oʻlchanadi. Taxminan 4 dS/m dan yuqorida koʻp ekinlar hosil yoʻqotadi; gʻoʻza va arpa loviya yoki makkajoʻxoriga qaraganda shoʻrga chidamliroq. | Засоление измеряют по удельной электропроводности насыщенной почвенной пасты (ECe, дСм/м). Выше примерно 4 дСм/м многие культуры теряют урожай; хлопчатник и ячмень переносят засоление лучше, чем фасоль или кукуруза. |
| `glossary.et` | The water a well-watered reference grass would lose to the air through evaporation and plant transpiration in a day, in mm. | `glossary.soil_moisture` | How much wetter or drier the root zone is than usual for that month.
